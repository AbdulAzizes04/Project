Problem Statement & Motivation

Sleep disorders such as Insomnia and Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) are widespread medical conditions associated with severe downstream morbidities, including hypertension, coronary artery disease, stroke, metabolic dysfunction, and cognitive impairment. In clinical environments, formal diagnosis requires in-laboratory Polysomnography (PSG), which is expensive, labor-intensive, and inaccessible for widespread screening.

Existing machine learning systems operate as opaque "black boxes," issuing classification labels without clinical justification. This project presents a transparent, clinical decision-support framework that leverages Stratified Multi-Model Machine Learning, SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations), and Generative Health Guidance (Google Gemini) to deliver interpretable, actionable sleep assessments.

Mathematical Foundations of SHAP

SHAP utilizes cooperative game theory (Shapley values) to fairly distribute the prediction payout among input features:

φi(x) = ∑S ⊆ F \ {i}   [ |S|! (|F| - |S| - 1)! / |F|! ]   [ fx(S ∪ {i}) - fx(S) ]

Where F is the total set of clinical features, S is a coalition subset of features excluding feature i, and fx(S) represents the model's expected prediction conditional on subset S. This guarantees local accuracy, missingness, and consistency.
