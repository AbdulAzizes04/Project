Project Overview

Why Explainable AI in Sleep Medicine?

Sleep disorders affect over 30% of adults globally, yet traditional black-box AI algorithms fail clinical adoption because clinicians and patients cannot see why a decision was reached. This framework bridges that gap.

Multi-Model Benchmarking

Systematically evaluates Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Support Vector Machine, Random Forest, and XGBoost using Stratified 5-Fold Cross-Validation on authentic clinical data.

SHAP Transparency (XAI)

Applies Shapley Additive exPlanations to quantify exact feature attributions for every individual inference. Reveals whether factors push risk up or act protectively.

Gemini AI Guidance

Translates raw ML metrics and SHAP factors into actionable, empathetic preventive sleep hygiene routines and lifestyle interventions using Google Gemini API guardrails.

Architectural Workflow

The 4-Stage Decision Support Pipeline

1
Clinical Input

User inputs physiological parameters: Age, BP, Sleep Duration, Sleep Quality, BMI, and Physical Activity.

2
ML Classification

Trained champion model generates multi-class prediction probabilities for None, Insomnia, and Sleep Apnea.

3
SHAP Attribution

TreeExplainer maps exact Shapley values for all input features to uncover causal risk and protective drivers.

4
Actionable Guidance

Gemini generates personalized sleep hygiene routines, and users can download an official clinical PDF report.
